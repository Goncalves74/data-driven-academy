A different approach

More Than a Power BI Course

This program is designed around operational knowledge, data quality and management decisions, rather than simply teaching software menus and isolated charts.

The value is not only in the tool.

The value is in understanding how information flows through an organization, how industrial indicators are defined and how reliable dashboards support faster and more consistent decisions.

01

Operational Experience

Concepts connected to real production, logistics, quality, maintenance and continuous improvement challenges.

02

Data Quality First

Learn to identify errors, inconsistencies, duplicates and missing information before building management indicators.

03

Centralized Information

Build a structured and reliable model that replaces disconnected spreadsheets with a common view of operational performance.

04

Management Decisions

Move from visualizing historical data to identifying deviations, understanding causes and supporting concrete actions.

Course framework

What You Will Learn

A complete learning journey from raw operational data to an integrated information system prepared for management analysis.

01

Business Requirements

Define the decisions, business questions, indicators, data sources and expected outcomes before starting the report.

02

Power Query

Connect, clean, combine and transform Excel, CSV, database and operational data through repeatable processes.

03

Data Quality

Create visible controls for missing values, duplicates, rejected records and failed data relationships.

04

Data Modelling

Build clear fact and dimension tables using a star schema designed for reliable analysis.

05

DAX and Measures

Develop reusable calculations, comparison measures, time intelligence and industrial performance indicators.

06

Industrial KPI

Work with production, quality, logistics, maintenance, reliability and operational performance metrics.

07

Dashboard Design

Create clear executive pages with visual hierarchy, useful navigation and information that supports action.

08

Publishing and Governance

Understand workspaces, access, refresh, security and the responsibilities associated with delivering trusted information.
